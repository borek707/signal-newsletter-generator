## Process

1. Select **one** Debate story with genuine controversy and business implications.
2. Select **one** Trenches story with concrete production context and actionable solution.
3. Select **exactly three** Signals that are breaking changes, deprecations, or major shifts.
4. Select 3-5 items for In the Know that do NOT duplicate Debate/Trenches/Signals.
5. Select Top Resources: one repo, one article/tool, one paper.
6. Write a short rationale for each selection.

Deduplication rule: a topic that appears in Debate or Trenches does NOT appear in Signals, Community Voice, or In the Know.

## Outputs

- `output/selection.md` — final lineup with rationale.
- `output/today-bullets.md` — 6-8 bullets for Today section.

## Review gate

Human approves the lineup before drafting.
