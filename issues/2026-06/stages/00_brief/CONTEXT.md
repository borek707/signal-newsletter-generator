## Inputs

- Month and year for the issue.
- Issue number (sequence from previous issues).
- Any human-defined themes or hooks.
- `../../_config/newsletter-types.md` — for special edition decisions.

## Process

Write a concise brief that answers:

1. What is the issue number and publish target date?
2. What is the main theme or hook for the month?
3. What are 2-3 candidate architecture debates?
4. What are 2-3 candidate trenches stories?
5. What are 5-8 candidate signals (breaking changes/major shifts)?
6. Are there any known events or constraints affecting selection?
7. Is this a standard monthly issue or a special edition?

Keep the brief short. Do not research yet — just frame what to look for.

## Outputs

- `output/brief.md` — the issue brief.

## Review gate

Human approves brief before research starts.
