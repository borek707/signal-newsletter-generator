# Image prompts, update after the 2026-10-14 lineup change

`images.md` stays valid for the Debate and the Trenches. This file adds prompts for the two new
items and replaces the cover, because the issue now leads with the agent swarms and GitLab rather
than NATS and Firebase. Same house style: raster PNG, warm off-white paper, rough black marker
lines, black sticky figures, red as the only accent.

## Cover (replaces the cover in `images.md`)

**Prompt:** Wide 16:9 SIGNAL cover in the existing Scalac diagram-comic style. Warm off-white background, rough black marker outlines, black sticky figures and system symbols, red as the only accent. Three small safety devices in a row, each with a red crack running through it. Left: a door with a sign `READ-ONLY`, slightly ajar. Centre: a big switch labelled `FAILOVER`, with two identical database cylinders behind it, both labelled `PRIMARY`. Right: a power switch labelled `CLEAN SHUTDOWN`. A single red line of damage enters through each device and continues past it. Large title: `THE SAFE PATH`. Small red subtitle: `SEPTEMBER SIGNAL`. Bottom line: `CHECK WHAT YOUR SAFETY MECHANISM CARRIES.` Generous blank space. No logos, gradients, photorealism or futuristic robot imagery.

## Follow-up from Issue #5: "Read-Only" turned into code execution

**Prompt:** Wide 16:9 diagram-comic in the existing Scalac style. Warm off-white background, thick black sketch lines, black sticky figures, red accents only. Left: a sticky figure inside a box labelled `SANDBOX`, at a small window with a sign `GET ONLY`. Through the window it passes a long paper strip made of numbered link tags `1`, `2`, `3` … `900`, each tag tied to the next. The strip runs to a box on the right labelled `SCREENSHOT SERVICE`, shaped like an old camera. The camera's flash is a red lightning bolt labelled `RUNS THE CODE`, and a red arrow from the flash reaches a server rack labelled `PRODUCTION`. A small second arrow leaves the sandbox through a pipe labelled `DNS`. Top title: `READ-ONLY WAS ENOUGH.` Bottom line: `ALLOWLIST EGRESS BY DESTINATION.` No logos, no company names, gradients, photorealism or generic robots.
