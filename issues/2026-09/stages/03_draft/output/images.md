# Image prompts — SIGNAL Issue #7

All three are for email as well as the blog: raster PNG on white-ish paper, no dark-mode dependency (Gmail strips SVG).

## Architecture Debate: A model that only decides

**Prompt:** Wide 16:9 diagram-comic matching the existing Scalac illustration style. Warm off-white paper background, rough black marker outlines, solid black sticky figures and red as the only accent. Split into two panels. Left panel labelled `DECIDE`: a sticky figure holds up a card with three tick boxes `ROUTE A / ROUTE B / ROUTE C`; a small box labelled `DECISION MODEL` instantly stamps a red tick on `ROUTE B` with a tiny red `0.93` beside it. Right panel labelled `JUDGE`: the same figure hands a long page labelled `RUBRIC` to a bigger box labelled `LLM`, which slowly writes a paragraph back. Between the panels a red dial labelled `THRESHOLD` with the needle off-centre, not at the middle. Top title: `DECIDE OR JUDGE?` Bottom line: `TUNE THE THRESHOLD BEFORE YOU TRUST IT.` No logos, gradients, photorealism, dense UI or generic robots.

## Notes from the Trenches: A clean shutdown that deleted data

**Prompt:** Wide 16:9 diagram-comic in the same existing Scalac style. Warm off-white background, thick black sketch lines, black sticky figures and red accents only. Three steps left to right, joined by black arrows. Step 1 `SHUTDOWN`: a sticky figure calmly presses a switch labelled `CLEAN STOP`; next to it a long file strip labelled `TIME INDEX 10 MB`, its right part filled with zeros, and scissors cutting it short. A red speech bubble from the file: `NO FSYNC`. Step 2 `RESTART`: the file strip is back at full length, zeros returned. Step 3 `RETENTION`: a calendar torn to `1970` sits on a fresh segment box; a red broom labelled `RETENTION` sweeps the box away while the figure looks back in surprise. Top title: `THE SAFE PATH LOST THE DATA.` Bottom line: `TEST THE CLEAN RESTART, NOT ONLY THE CRASH.` No logos, gradients, dashboards or generic AI robots.

## Cover: The damage took the safe path

One idea unifies the issue, so a cover is justified.

**Prompt:** Wide 16:9 SIGNAL cover in the same existing Scalac diagram-comic style. Warm off-white background, black sticky figures and system symbols, red as the only accent. Three small safety devices in a row, each with a red crack running through it. Left: a power switch labelled `CLEAN SHUTDOWN`. Centre: a plug that reconnects itself labelled `AUTO-RECOVER`, with a tiny note `STABLE`. Right: a big red-handled lever labelled `KILL SWITCH`. A single red line of damage enters through each device and continues past it. Large title: `THE SAFE PATH`. Small red subtitle: `SEPTEMBER SIGNAL`. Bottom line: `CHECK WHAT YOUR SAFETY MECHANISM CARRIES.` Generous blank space. No logos, gradients, photorealism or futuristic robot imagery.
