## Process

1. Select **one** Debate story with genuine controversy and business implications.
2. Select **one** Trenches story with concrete production context and actionable solution.
3. Select **exactly three** Signals that are breaking changes, deprecations, or major shifts.
4. Select 3-5 items for In the Know that do NOT duplicate Debate/Trenches/Signals.
5. Select Top Resources: one repo, one article/tool, one paper.
6. Write a short rationale for each selection.

Deduplication rule: a topic that appears in Debate or Trenches does NOT appear in Signals, Community Voice, or In the Know.

## Outputs

- `output/selection.md` — final lineup with rationale.
- `output/today-bullets.md` — 6-8 bullets for Today section.

## What to hand the human before drafting starts

Drafting is the expensive stage. Put all of this in front of the human first, and get an explicit go-ahead:

1. The candidate register from `01_research` with scores and `SELECT` / `CONTEXT` / `REJECT` decisions.
2. The recommended issue theme, in one or two sentences.
3. The proposed lineup, section by section.
4. Rejected topics, each with a one-line reason. Duplicates of earlier issues and wrong-month items belong here explicitly, so the human can overrule if they disagree.
5. Every source tagged `primary`, `verification`, or `community`.
6. Editorial questions **only** where two genuinely comparable directions would produce materially different issues. Do not manufacture a choice to look thorough, and do not ask about anything the brief or the rules already settle.

## Review gate

Human approves the lineup before drafting.
