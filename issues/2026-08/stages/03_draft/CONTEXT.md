## Illustration prompts

During drafting, create `output/images.md` with two required prompts: Architecture
Debate and Notes from the Trenches. Add an optional cover prompt only when one idea
unifies the issue. Match the existing Scalac diagram-comic style: warm off-white
background, rough black linework, black sticky figures and red as the only accent.
Short hand-lettered text is allowed when it makes the lesson clear at a glance.
