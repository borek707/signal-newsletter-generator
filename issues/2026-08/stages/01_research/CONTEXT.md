## Research scope

Research August 2026 changes across Scala, JVM, Kafka, distributed systems and
AI systems. Use official release notes, JEPs, primary engineering posts and
original papers where available. Social sources serve as leads, not evidence.

Use `agent-reach` for external research. If its reader cannot fetch a public page,
use Scrapling as a fallback without bypassing authentication, paywalls, CAPTCHA or
access controls. Use `last30days` as a small social-discovery pass. Record the
originating event date and assign `NEW_THIS_MONTH`, `MATERIAL_UPDATE` or
`BACKGROUND_ONLY` to every candidate.
